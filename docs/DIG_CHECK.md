# Check Before You Dig — awareness prototype

Select a campus in the header, choose a point or draw a simple polygon by clicking the map, specify a 0–500 m buffer and run the check. Online Supabase mode queries PostGIS geography against the actual recorded geometries in the selected campus, under the caller's RLS. Demo mode uses a spherical approximation against local demo records and is not authoritative. A maximum of 200 results is displayed, with a warning if more match. Open a result to inspect available photos and detail.

**This is not excavation clearance.** An empty result does not establish the absence of utilities or safety. Data can be incomplete, inaccurate, outdated, not synced, or invisible to the caller, including other users' drafts. Nearby assets assigned to another campus are excluded. Map placement and buffer distances do not account for survey error, asset width, future changes, or site conditions. Consult utility owners, obtain permits and verify on site before work. The check is online-only in Supabase mode; local queued changes are not included.
